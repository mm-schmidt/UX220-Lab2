The Best Thing I've Read Recently...

Was the book Braiding Sweetgrass by Robin Wall Kimmerer. The book explores the relationship between humans and the land from the perspective of Wall Kimmerer, and American Botanist and citizen of the Potawatomi Nation. Kimmerer recounts personal stories, shares Native American knowledge and myths, and describes Western botanical science to promote joyful, sustainable land stewardship.

Link: https://robinwallkimmerer.com/book/braiding-sweetgrass/
